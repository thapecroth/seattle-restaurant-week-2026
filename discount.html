How maximum savings is calculated

For each event offer, select a valid basket: one permitted choice per course, with the published quantities, sharing rules and supplements. “Up to” describes the highest-value documented choice basket here, not a saving on every combination. It is not a certified global maximum when allowed alternatives have unverified prices.

Percentage saved: (regular equivalent price − event menu price) ÷ regular equivalent price. Dollar savings are normalized to the published intended number of diners. Missing serving counts do not become assumed individual portions.

If a cheaper identical normal combo is available, that price takes precedence over a more expensive à-la-carte decomposition. Happy-hour alternatives stay separately labeled and conditional where their SRW availability is unknown. Negative savings remain visible: an event set is not automatically a bargain.

Verified comparison: exact branch, current first-party dine-in prices, a fully priced basket and established equal portions. Estimate: a complete, primary-source named-item price basket with unresolved portion or ordering-channel differences. No documented price route means no numerical discount—not “0%.”

These are menu-price comparisons, not guaranteed bill savings or food-quality scores. Fee differences and normal-special availability are disclosed with the offer. Original source links and checked dates are included.
